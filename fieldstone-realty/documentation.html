Fieldstone — Real Estate Agency Landing Page

Thank you for your purchase. This guide covers everything you need to put the template online with your own listings. No build tools are required: open index.html in a browser and you're running.

Change colours and fonts

Open assets/css/style.css and edit the variables in :root:

:root {
  --ink: #1d1f1c;    /* text, headings, dark section */
  --cobalt: #2a4fd6; /* buttons, links, front doors in the illustrations */
  --tint: #eef0f5;   /* tinted sections */
  --clay: #f1d3c8;   /* warm accent */
}

To change fonts, replace the Google Fonts <link> in index.html and update --font-display and --font-body.

Add your listings

All listings come from LISTINGS at the top of assets/js/main.js. Each one looks like this:

{ address: "12 Linden Avenue", area: "Westbrook", price: 865000,
  type: "house", beds: 4, baths: 2, size: 2140,
  badge: "New", art: "gabled", sky: "#dfe6f6", body: "#f1d3c8",
  image: "assets/img/12-linden.jpg" }
  • type: house, townhouse or apartment (used by the search filter).
  • badge: a short label such as "New", "Open home Sat" or "Sold". Leave empty ("") for none.
  • image: add a photo path to show a real photo. Landscape photos around 1200 × 900 px work best.
  • Without a photo, an illustration is drawn: art chooses the style (gabled, modern, terrace, block, cottage), sky is the background colour and body the wall colour.

Set CURRENCY and AREA_UNIT (for example "m²") at the top of the same file. Remember to update the price options in the hero search to match your market.

Mortgage calculator

The default values and limits are on the range inputs in the MORTGAGE CALCULATOR section of index.html (min, max, step, value). The calculator shows principal and interest only; the page text already tells visitors that taxes and insurance are extra.

Receive viewing requests and messages

Both forms validate and show a confirmation, but don't send anything yet. Sign up for a form service such as Formspree, Getform or Netlify Forms and add its URL to the contact form:

<form class="contact-form" novalidate data-contact-form
      action="https://formspree.io/f/your-id" method="POST">

For the viewing dialog, the simplest option is to send the request with the form service's JavaScript example inside the submit handler in main.js, or to replace the "Request a viewing" button with a link to your booking calendar.

Agent photos

<div class="headshot" data-initials="DO"
     style="background-image:url('assets/img/daniel.jpg')"
     role="img" aria-label="Photo of Daniel Osei"></div>

Square photos around 800 × 800 px work best.

Before you publish

  • Replace the example listings, agents, phone numbers and address.
  • Remove "Listings shown are examples." from the footer.
  • Add any licence number or legal text your local real estate rules require.

Publish

Upload the whole folder to any static host: Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or the public_html folder of regular web hosting.
